Tech Career Roadmap Platform

A web platform that helps computer science students answer one hard question: which tech track should I choose? I led the team and delivered the first working prototype.

EdTech Team leader Working prototype
Discuss on LinkedIn Back to portfolio

The problem

Most CS students reach their second or third year without knowing which tech track fits them: web development, mobile, data science, AI, security, networks. The information exists, but it is scattered across hundreds of videos and articles with no clear order. Students lose months jumping between resources instead of following a path.

Personal angle: as a CS student myself at EELU, this was my own problem before it became a project. I built the platform I wished I had on day one.

What the platform does

Organized learning tracks

Each tech track is presented as a structured path, from fundamentals to job-ready skills.

Clear roadmaps

Step-by-step roadmaps that show what to learn, in what order, and why.

Curated free resources

The best free content from YouTube and learning platforms, selected per step instead of endless searching.

Track comparison

Helps a student compare tracks and pick the one that matches their interests before committing months to it.

My role

  • Led the team: divided the work, kept everyone moving, and owned the final integration.
  • Delivered the first working prototype of the platform.
  • Shaped the content structure: how tracks, roadmaps and resources connect so a student never asks "what now?".
