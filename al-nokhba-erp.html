ERP System for Al Nokhba Stone

A complete web-based ERP for an Egyptian marble & granite company, covering the full production cycle from raw blocks to export payments. 17 modules, 11 user roles, and a full Arabic RTL dashboard - running entirely in the browser with no backend.

17 modules 11 roles Arabic RTL UI No backend - localStorage

Why this system exists

I worked at Al Nokhba Stone in export sales and marketing, so I saw the daily reality: raw blocks arriving from quarries, slabs tracked by hand, export quotations built in scattered files, and accounting done apart from everything else. I designed and built this ERP to put that whole cycle in one system, in Arabic, for the people who actually run it.

Raw blocks→ Cutting & manufacturing→ Quality→ Slab inventory→ Sales & export→ Collections→ Accounting & reports

What it covers

Purchasing & raw blocks

Receiving raw blocks from quarries, supplier records and purchasing costs.

Manufacturing

Cutting and production stages with cost tracking per stage.

Inventory & slab tracking

Raw blocks and finished slabs, with individual slab tracking and FIFO / weighted-average inventory valuation.

Export orders & quotations

Quotations and export orders for international clients.

CRM & invoicing

Customer records, follow-ups and invoices in one place.

Accounting & HR

Collections and payments, plus HR records for the team.

Financial reports

  • Profit & loss, balance sheet and cash-flow reports generated from the system's own data.
  • Interactive dashboards with charts, in both dark and light modes.

Technical choices

No backend, by design. The whole system runs in the browser and uses localStorage as its database. For a small company that means zero hosting cost, zero server maintenance, and data that stays on the machine used every day.
  • Full Arabic right-to-left interface, built for the team's daily use rather than translated as an afterthought.
  • 11 user roles so each person sees the modules their job needs.
  • Built with plain JavaScript, HTML and CSS - no framework lock-in, easy to hand over and extend.
